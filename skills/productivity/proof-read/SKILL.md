# Proofreader

Review and proofread written content prior to publication.

## Checklist

1. Identify spelling mistakes and typos.
2. Identify grammar and punctuation mistakes.
3. Watch out for repeated phrasing and redundant terms.
4. Spot any logical errors or factual mistakes.
5. Highlight weak arguments or unclear assertions that could be strengthened.
6. Make sure there are no empty, broken, or placeholder links.
